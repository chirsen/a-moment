简体中文

片刻（英文名称 A Moment）是一款 macOS 休息与喝水提醒应用。

本地处理的信息

应用在本机保存提醒设置、自定义提醒名称与内容、使用电脑的活跃时长、提醒到点与完成次数、跳过和未回应次数、习惯图标、目标及归档时间。这些信息用于计时、展示提醒和习惯统计。

应用读取系统提供的空闲时间、屏幕锁定/睡眠状态及演示相关状态，以决定何时提醒；不会记录键盘输入的内容、聊天文字、屏幕图像或浏览历史。

数据传输与第三方

应用无需注册账号，不包含广告或分析 SDK，也不会把使用记录传到运营者服务器。核心计时与统计在本机完成。

领取免费试用、购买永久解锁和恢复购买通过 Apple StoreKit 处理，需要使用 Apple 账户。应用在本机验证 Apple 提供的交易凭证，并读取商品标识、交易标识、原始购买日期及撤销状态，用于判断 30 天试用期限和永久使用权限。付款及账户信息由 Apple 处理，应用不读取银行卡信息，也不将交易记录上传至运营者服务器。删除本机统计不会重置 Apple 账户的试用期限或购买记录。

你可以主动选择文件位置导出 JSON 备份。若选择 iCloud Drive，文件由你的 Apple 账号与 iCloud 服务处理；应用目前不提供多设备自动同步。导出的文件包含你的自定义提醒信息与历史数据，请自行决定保存与分享对象。

饮水提示中提供 WHO 网站链接。主动打开链接后，由浏览器访问第三方网站，该网站适用其自身的隐私政策。

若主动联系支持邮箱，你提供的邮件内容和联系信息仅用于回复与处理你的请求。

本支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，不添加广告、分析脚本或追踪 Cookie。GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者 IP 地址，详见 GitHub Pages 说明及 GitHub 隐私声明。这与应用内本地保存的习惯记录分开处理。

保存、恢复与删除

数据保存在本机应用数据目录中。近期明细保留最近 400 个有记录日期，更早数据汇入累计。删除提醒会将对应习惯归档，不会自动删除历史记录。

导入备份会替换当前统计，并先保存一份恢复副本。导出文件、恢复副本以及自行存入 iCloud Drive 的备份需要你分别管理。需要删除本机全部数据时，请先退出应用，并参考支持页面的数据删除步骤；仅删除 App 本体不一定删除数据或备份。

联系与更新

隐私问题请联系 chirsen@163.com。若功能或数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本政策与商店隐私说明。

English

A Moment (片刻 in Simplified Chinese) is a macOS app for break and water reminders.

Information processed on your Mac

The app stores reminder settings, custom reminder names and messages, active computer time, due and completed reminder counts, skips and unanswered reminders, habit icons, goals, and archive dates. These records support timers, reminders, and habit statistics.

The app reads system-provided idle time, lock/sleep state, and presentation-related state to decide when to show reminders. It does not record typed content, conversations, screen images, or browsing history.

Transfers and third parties

No app account registration is required. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not upload usage records to the operator's servers. Core timing and statistics run locally.

Claiming a free trial, buying permanent access and restoring purchases use Apple StoreKit and your Apple Account. The app verifies Apple-provided transactions locally and reads the product and transaction identifiers, original purchase date and revocation status to determine the 30-day trial period and permanent access. Apple handles payment and account information. The app does not access payment card information or upload transaction records to the operator's servers. Deleting local statistics does not reset the trial period or purchase records associated with your Apple Account.

You may export a JSON backup to a location you choose. If you choose iCloud Drive, Apple handles that file through your Apple account and iCloud service. The app does not currently offer automatic cross-device sync. Exported files contain your custom reminder information and history; you decide where to store them and with whom to share them.

Hydration tips include a link to the WHO website. Opening it visits a third-party website in your browser, subject to that website's privacy policy.

If you email support, the information you voluntarily provide is used to respond to and handle your request.

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Pages documentation and privacy statement. This website processing is separate from the habit records kept locally in the app.

Retention, restoration, and deletion

Records are stored in the app's local data directory. Detailed history retains the most recent 400 dates with records; older records are included in lifetime totals. Deleting a reminder archives the habit without deleting its history.

Importing a backup replaces current statistics after saving a recovery copy. Exported files, recovery copies, and backups you place in iCloud Drive must be managed separately. To remove all local data, quit the app first and follow the support page instructions. Deleting only the app may leave its data and backups behind.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact chirsen@163.com. If features or data practices change, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details.
