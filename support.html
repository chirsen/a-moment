简体中文

  • 免费试用：免费下载后，在首页领取「30 天试用」，从 Apple 确认领取时起连续计算 30 天。到期不自动扣款；提醒和预览停止，历史记录仍可查看、导入和导出。
  • 永久解锁：在「试用与购买」中一次付费，永久使用全部提醒。中国大陆 ¥9.90，美国 $1.49，以购买页面显示的 App Store 价格为准。没有订阅费用。
  • 恢复试用或购买：App Store 登录原 Apple 账户，在「试用与购买」点「恢复购买」。换电脑或重装不会重新计算 30 天；统计备份不包含购买权限。首次领取、购买和恢复需要连接 App Store，已验证的权限可由 StoreKit 本地记录恢复；价格暂时加载失败不会取消已有权限。
  • 到期后重新解锁：提醒恢复后，喝水从下一次活跃使用开始新周期，不补弹未解锁期间的提醒。
  • 调整提醒：在「此刻」页点击提醒旁的「…」。可调整间隔、预览；自定义提醒还可选图标。
  • 切换语言：macOS「系统设置 → 通用 → 语言与地区 → 应用程序」中添加片刻，选择简体中文或英语，重启应用。
  • 提前通知没出现：检查「系统设置 → 通知 → 片刻」，以及当前专注模式。应用内提示与系统通知是不同的呈现方式。
  • 投屏时不提醒：检测到镜像投屏或全屏演示时会暂停弹出提示，结束后稍等再试。
  • 金色标识：完成个人每日目标即可获得。休息当天无跳过也可获得金叶；调整当前目标会更新历史金标，不改变原始次数。
  • 备份与换电脑：「习惯」页右上角数据按钮导出 JSON。新 Mac 上导入；可手动通过 iCloud Drive 传递。没有自动云同步。
  • 从测试版转到商店版：先在测试版导出，再在商店版导入；沙盒版的数据目录与旧版不同。
  • 删除全部本机数据：先退出应用，再在 Finder「前往文件夹」中进入 ~/Library/Containers/com.chirsen.healthremind/Data/Library/Application Support/HealthRemind/，确认是本应用目录后移除其中的设置、统计及 Backups。删除不可恢复，请按需要先导出。旧非沙盒测试版目录为 ~/Library/Application Support/HealthRemind/。你自行保存到其他位置或 iCloud Drive 的备份需要另外删除。

English

  • Free trial: Download for free and claim the 30-day Trial from the main window. The 30 consecutive days begin with the original Apple-confirmed transaction. No automatic charge follows. At expiry, reminders and previews stop; history viewing, import and export remain available.
  • Permanent unlock: Make a one-time purchase in Trial & Purchases for all reminders. China mainland ¥9.90; US $1.49. Check the localized App Store price shown before purchase. There is no subscription.
  • Restore a trial or purchase: Sign in to the App Store with the original Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases in Trial & Purchases. Moving Macs or reinstalling does not restart the trial. History backups do not contain purchase access. Claiming, buying and restoring require an App Store connection; StoreKit can restore verified access from its local records. A price-loading failure does not cancel existing access.
  • Unlock after expiry: Water timing starts a new cycle at the next active use. Reminders from the locked period are not replayed.
  • Adjust reminders: On Now, use the “…” button beside a reminder. Change its interval or preview it; custom reminders also support icon selection.
  • Choose a language: In macOS System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications, add A Moment and choose English or Simplified Chinese. Relaunch the app.
  • Missing advance notifications: Check System Settings → Notifications → A Moment and your Focus settings. System notifications and the app's own reminders are separate.
  • Quiet during presentations: Detected display mirroring and full-screen presentations suppress reminders. Allow a short cooldown after ending them.
  • Gold marks: Meet your personal daily goal to earn one. A day without skipped breaks also earns a gold leaf. Changing current goals updates past gold marks without changing actual counts.
  • Back up or move Macs: Export JSON using the data button in Habits, then import it on another Mac. You can transfer backups manually through iCloud Drive. Automatic sync is not available.
  • Move from a development build: Export from the old app, then import into the App Store version. Their data directories differ.
  • Delete all local data: Quit the app. In Finder → Go → Go to Folder, open ~/Library/Containers/com.chirsen.healthremind/Data/Library/Application Support/HealthRemind/. After checking that this is A Moment's directory, remove its settings, statistics, and Backups folder. This is irreversible; export first if needed. Older non-sandboxed development builds use ~/Library/Application Support/HealthRemind/. Remove any backups you saved elsewhere or in iCloud Drive separately.

Contact chirsen@163.com with the app version, macOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Redact personal information from screenshots; you do not need to send your complete habit history.
